About

I'm a cybersecurity undergraduate at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology, specializing in network security, penetration testing and secure application development. Outside coursework I spend most of my time in TryHackMe rooms and building full-stack projects — usually the two intersect, since I like understanding a system well enough to secure it, which means building one first.
