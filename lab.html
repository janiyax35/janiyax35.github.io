    • [✓] Content-Security-Policy: scripts limited to self and 3 pinned CDNs
    • [✓] Subresource Integrity (sha384) on every third-party script and stylesheet
    • [✓] No inline scripts, no eval, no frameworks, no build step
    • [✓] No analytics, no trackers, no third-party cookies
    • [✓] RFC 9116 /.well-known/security.txt
    • [✓] HTTPS enforced · strict referrer policy · rel="noopener" on external links

    • [!] GitHub Pages can't send custom headers, so CSP is set by meta tag and frame-ancestors isn't enforced
